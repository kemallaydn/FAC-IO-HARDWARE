Robotistan PCBA assembly instructions — quotation/review draft

Deliverable requested: complete assembled FAC-IO Mini V1 board, with all BOM
parts populated and the WIZnet WIZ850io Ethernet module installed in J6/J7.
Do not ship EXT1 loose for customer installation.

WIZ850io sourcing reference: LCSC C134462, manufacturer WIZnet, MPN WIZ850io.
The parent PCB has two factory-installed Samtec SSQ-106-03-G-S sockets. After
PCB assembly and inspection, insert the WIZ850io module into J6/J7 in the
documented orientation and verify the module sits fully in both sockets. This
is a low-volume manual/THT final assembly operation; it is not represented as a
57th PCB centroid.

Before order acceptance, confirm that Robotistan can source C134462 and perform
this module insertion. The exact catalog codes for C1, C2, L1, SW1 and SW2 are
still unresolved in the online BOM. Quote exact manufacturer MPNs first; do not
substitute without written engineering approval and synchronized CAD/BOM.

This board order does not include firmware flashing, network provisioning,
security-key provisioning, enclosure or first-article functional acceptance.
